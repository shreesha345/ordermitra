Before this, every order started with a phone call. And those calls were long. Someone had to check the stock by hand. See if the medicine was available. Check the price. Compare it. Review it. Then write the bill, and send the GST details. All of that, for just one order. It took far too much time.

So we asked one simple question. What if an AI agent did all of this for us? That is OrderMitra. It lives inside WhatsApp, the app every pharmacy already uses. Nothing new to learn.

Here is how it works. The pharmacy just sends their order. It can be a photo of a handwritten list. A PDF. A voice note, in English, Hindi, Kannada, Tamil, or Telugu. Or simple text, and here I am using Wispr Flow, so I just speak and it types for me. The AI reads it, matches every medicine, and checks our stock. If something is short, it offers the same medicine from another brand. The pharmacy taps confirm, and in seconds they get a GST invoice, with a discount if they pay today, and a Pay button right there in the chat.

Now the other side. This is the dashboard for the wholesaler. The Today page shows what needs attention: new orders, today's sales, and reminders you can send in one click. Open any order, and you can see exactly what the AI did, step by step. Nothing is hidden.

Next, the app checks every order for you. It looks at how much the pharmacy already owes. It also checks their drug licence. If something is wrong, the order stops and waits for the owner to decide. Each pharmacy also gets a grade, from A to D, based on how quickly they pay.

Then there is stock. The app knows every batch, and when it expires. So you always see which medicines are about to expire. It also shows what customers asked for, but you did not have. And the What to Buy page tells you what to order next, and why.

Filing GST used to be a headache. Here, it is one click. The app builds your GSTR-1 for the month as an Excel file, ready to upload. It even tells you what to fix before you file.

And if you ever have a question, just ask. Type, who owes me the most, and it tells you. Type, add one hundred strips of Dolo, and it updates your stock. Made a mistake? Tap undo.

Now, you may wonder, can we trust an AI with all this? Yes, because we kept it simple. The AI only reads the message and suggests what to do. The price, the GST, and the credit are all worked out by the app, not the AI. And for any big change, the owner has to say yes first.

So that is OrderMitra. The order comes in on WhatsApp. The bill goes out in seconds. And your team just checks and sends. Less time on calls, more time on business.
